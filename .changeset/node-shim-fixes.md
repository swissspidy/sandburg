Node compatibility fixes in the runtime: `fs.openSync` decodes numeric and `r+` flags as open(2) does (no truncation without `O_TRUNC`, `O_EXCL` honoured), `writeFile` takes `wx`/`a`/`r+` flags, `fs.read`/`readSync` take an options object, `fs.write`'s callback gets the buffer, file streams emit `open` and `ready`, and `unwatchFile` stops only the listener named. A throwing `setImmediate` callback no longer drops the rest of its batch, `Timeout.refresh()` after a timeout fired keeps the process alive, `process.exit()` in an `exit` listener ends the process with its code, `process.exit(undefined)` and integer-string exit codes behave as in Node, and `process.exit()` (also in a request handler) ends the process once: its timers stop, and its servers' and WebSockets' open requests and connections are answered or closed once. `net` sockets end their side when the peer does (`allowHalfOpen: false`, as in Node), so programs that use them exit; a killed `shell: true` child reports its signal. On the host, stored compiles are keyed by the esbuild version, a cache bundle made while its cache was rewritten is not kept, and requests are answered with an error once the runtime crashes or is stopped, or when the program exits before its own server answered them (its child processes' servers keep answering theirs).
